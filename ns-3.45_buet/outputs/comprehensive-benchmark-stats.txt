================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. **Small Networks (20 nodes):**
   - ECC-AODV excels in mobile scenarios (PDR=78.34%)
   - Standard AODV still optimal for stable static networks (PDR=85.80%)
   - ECC-AODV provides 2.48% PDR improvement over AODV in mobile case

2. **Medium Networks (30 nodes):**
   - ECC-AODV DOMINATES in mobile scenario: PDR=87.82% (+5.14% vs AODV)
   - Throughput peak: 289.96Kbps (ECC-AODV mobile)
   - CC-AODV competitive in mobile (PDR=84.19%)

3. **Large Networks (40 nodes):**
   - AODV maintains lead in mobile scenarios (PDR=78.80%)
   - CC-AODV best delay (144.15ms static)
   - ECC-AODV shows degradation at 40 nodes static (PDR=70.98%)
   - Scalability limit: ECC-AODV less effective with many nodes in static case

4. **Protocol Comparison:**
   - **Standard AODV**: Best for small, stable networks
   - **CC-AODV**: Good delay performance, moderate PDR
   - **ECC-AODV**: Excellent in medium networks with mobility (30 nodes, 4-10 m/s)

================================================================================
PARAMETER SENSITIVITY FOR ECC-AODV
