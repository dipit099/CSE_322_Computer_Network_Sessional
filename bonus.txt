Bonus Opportunities
You can earn bonus points in several areas:

Cross transmission of packets, i.e., transmission of packets from one type of node to that 
of another type (example: simulating a flow n1->n2->…->nx, where the first few nodes 
are wired and the rest nodes are wireless)

Measuring extra metrics: Examples include per-node throughput or the variation in queue size over time.
{ If possible. DOnt try too much changes to adapt these}

Brand new idea: Implementing a novel idea with good performance improvement that does not exist in current literature can earn a double bonus.
FOCUS ON THIS if possible.

Important Note on Performance:
It is not mandatory that your modified mechanism results in a performance improvement. The primary requirement is to clearly demonstrate what changes you made and explain the intuition behind them.
